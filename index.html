The practice

Four domains, one operating model.

Actionable intelligence isn't a tool. It's what happens when clinical, quality, predictive, and operational data are synthesized by someone who still rounds — and who sits in the steering committee the next morning.

01 — Clinical

Decision support built by a clinician who rounds.

Alerts people act on, because the workflow was designed at the bedside first.

Documentation

Documentation Intelligence

AutoCDI, discharge clarification, DNR accuracy. Capture that reflects the patient in front of you.

Content

Advisories & Order Sets

BPAs and order sets tuned against real firing and override data, not assumptions.

Surveillance

Early Warning Programs

Deterioration indices wired to a rapid-response pathway, with the response itself measured.

Capability

Physician Builder Programs

Standing up and running builder cohorts so clinicians can change their own EHR.

02 — Quality

Every index, every ranking, every opportunity.

Where the score comes from, what actually moves it, and in what order.

Benchmarking

Vizient Mortality, LOS & Cost

Observed-to-expected decomposition down to the service line and the chart.

CMS

Star Rating Improvement

Measure-level modeling of which interventions shift the composite, and by how much.

Reputation

US News Rankings Strategy

Specialty-level gap analysis against the methodology as published.

Market

Competitor & Opportunity Analysis

Public and internal data combined to size where improvement is worth the effort.

03 — Predictive

Models that reach the workflow, not the slide deck.

Thresholds, governance, and the human pathway that turns a score into an action.

Deployment

Model Deployment & Thresholds

Threshold selection tied to staffing reality and alert burden, then monitored for drift.

Governance

GenAI Implementation

Evaluation, oversight, and rollout structures that survive contact with a health system.

Ambient

Ambient AI Operationalization

Selection, pilot design, and adoption measurement beyond the vendor's dashboard.

Platform

HIPAA-Compliant LLM Services

Enterprise LLM access with retrieval over clinical data, inside the compliance boundary.

04 — Operational

Supply, throughput, and the data between systems.

The unglamorous integrations that decide whether anything else works.

Utilization

Supply & Utilization Analytics

Barcode, dispense, and nursing data joined to find the variation worth attacking.

Capacity

Throughput & Capacity Intelligence

ED, OR, and ICU flow modeled end to end rather than department by department.

Surveillance

Real-Time Operational Monitoring

Live dashboards that surface signal early — respiratory virus upticks ahead of CDC reporting.

Data

Integration & Reporting

MSSQL, Snowflake, and cloud pipelines that make the above answerable in one query.
