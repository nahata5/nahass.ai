Key projects & initiatives

2025
AutoCDI implementation
Multi-phased documentation strategy; CMI increased 0.4, mortality index reduced 1.2 → 0.7, ~$50M incremental revenue captured.
2025
HRRP reduction project
Rule-based system to identify, flag, and notify team members of pending readmissions — targeting care coordination in the ED and at discharge, using the Epic Risk of Readmission cognitive computing model.
2025
IMRM critical care mortality reduction
Integrated Epic's ICU Risk of Readmission or Mortality score into intensivist workflow for patients at high risk of ICU return.
2025
Physician Builder program leadership
Established and led an informatics program of 25+ Physician Builders and 45 SmartUsers.
2024 – 2025
Sepsis mortality initiative
Coding and clinical interventions targeting source control and bundle compliance; >3% absolute mortality reduction across 12 hospitals.
2024 – 2025
Physician Builder enhancements
Reduced CDI no-response rates, added discharge clarification notes, increased DNR documentation accuracy by 10%.
2024
Respiratory virus dashboard
Real-time MSSQL/Tableau tool detecting upticks two weeks ahead of CDC data, enabling timely masking mandates.
2024
IV fluid shortage management
System-wide dashboard achieving 44% fluid reduction while shortening length of stay; subject of national publication.
2024
Deterioration index rollout
Directed 12-hospital implementation associated with reduced mortality; recognized at Epic UGM and published in NEJM AI.
